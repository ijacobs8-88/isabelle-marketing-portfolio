About Me

I'm a marketing student at Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business and a student in Barrett, The Honors College. I'm especially interested in branding, advertising, social media, and understanding what connects consumers with a brand.

What I enjoy most about marketing is the combination of creativity and strategy. I like developing new ideas, understanding an audience, and using research and insights to make marketing decisions that have a purpose behind them.

Through my coursework, projects, and professional experiences, I have continued to build my skills in marketing strategy, content creation, consumer research, analytics, and communication.

What I'm Interested In

Branding & Advertising

I'm interested in the strategy and creativity behind building a recognizable brand, developing campaigns, and finding ways for brands to stand out and connect with their audiences.

Social Media & Content

I enjoy keeping up with social media trends and thinking about how brands can turn those trends into content that feels engaging, current, and authentic to their identity.

Consumer Insights

I'm interested in understanding why consumers make the choices they do and how research, data, and audience insights can help shape stronger marketing decisions.
